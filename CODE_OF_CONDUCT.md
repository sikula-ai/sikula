# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Respecting community purpose and gatherings
- Engaging kindly and honestly with others
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes
- Committing to repairing harm when it occurs
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment — violating expressed boundaries after clear requests to stop
- Insulting or derogatory comments and personal or character attacks
- Stereotyping or discrimination based on personal characteristics
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Threatening violence or harm
- Misleading others about your identity or impersonating another person
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at **contact@sikula.ai**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, with confidentiality maintained throughout.

### Enforcement Ladder

**1. Warning**
A private written warning for a single incident or series of minor incidents, clarifying expectations.

**2. Temporarily Limited Activities**
A time-limited cooldown for repeated violations or a more serious first offense.

**3. Temporary Suspension**
A temporary suspension from community participation for a repeated pattern or a single serious violation, with conditions for return.

**4. Permanent Ban**
Permanent removal for repeated violations unresolved by other measures, or for an extremely serious violation.
