# Sikula Contributor Privacy Notice

Version: 1.0  
Effective date: May 21, 2026  
Controller: Petr Lexa  
Contact: contact@sikula.ai

This notice explains how Sikula processes personal data connected with contributions and Contributor License Agreement records.

## 1. Data we process

We may process:

- GitHub username and profile information;
- name and email address if provided;
- pull request, issue, commit, repository, and contribution metadata;
- CLA acceptance timestamp;
- CLA version, CLA text, and cryptographic hash of the CLA text;
- IP address if collected by the signing system;
- related audit, compliance, and licensing records.

## 2. Why we process this data

We process this data to:

- manage contributions to Sikula;
- record acceptance of the Sikula Contributor License Agreement;
- verify rights granted for contributions;
- support open-source, commercial, proprietary, hosted, enterprise, and other licensing of Sikula;
- maintain legal, compliance, audit, tax, accounting, corporate, and due diligence records;
- establish, exercise, transfer, sublicense, license, enforce, defend, audit, or verify rights in Sikula, the CLA, or contributions.

## 3. Legal basis

Depending on the context, we process this data because it is necessary for:

- entering into or performing the Contributor License Agreement;
- legitimate interests in maintaining contribution, licensing, compliance, audit, and due diligence records;
- compliance with legal obligations where applicable.

## 4. Who may receive the data

We may share relevant records with:

- project maintainers;
- GitHub and CLA signing/automation providers;
- future Project Owners or successors;
- investors, acquirers, purchasers, merger partners, or other transaction parties;
- commercial licensees where reasonably necessary to verify their rights;
- lawyers, accountants, auditors, tax advisers, and other professional advisers;
- courts, authorities, or other recipients where required or reasonably necessary for legal reasons.

## 5. Retention

We may keep contribution and CLA records for as long as the Project exists and for as long as reasonably necessary afterwards for legal, licensing, compliance, audit, tax, accounting, corporate, transaction, and due diligence purposes.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, You may have rights to request access, rectification, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other rights in relation to Your personal data.

You may contact us at the contact address above.

If You are in the Czech Republic or the European Union, You may also have the right to contact the relevant data protection authority.

## 7. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The current version will be published in the Project repository or another location published by the Project Owner.
